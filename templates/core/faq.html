How is my jewelry shipped and insured?

Every Luckjary parcel is dispatched via FedEx Luxury Priority Overnight or armored white-glove transport. All packages are 100% insured up to \$100,000 against loss, theft, or damage during transit. Direct adult signature is mandatory upon delivery.

What is the Luckjary Lifetime Warranty?

We stand behind our craftsmanship forever. Our lifetime warranty covers prong tightening, ultrasonic cleaning, rhodium re-dipping, and manufacturing defect repairs for life. Simply contact our concierge team to schedule an atelier maintenance service.

Do you accept bespoke custom commissions?

Yes. Our master atelier crafts custom engagement rings, anniversary suites, and heirloom resets. Contact our concierge to begin a one-on-one consultation with a senior gemologist.
